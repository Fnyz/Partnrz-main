Investment
Criteria

We back early-stage technology & software founders with capital, network and hands-on support. We lead or co-lead and stay engaged post-investment. First response within two business days.

01

What We Invest In

High-conviction checks into early-stage tech & software companies where capital plus hands-on support accelerate inflection.

Focus areas & sweet spot

We lead or co-lead rounds where we can be an unfair advantage to the founding team.

Stage

Pre-seed to Series A (seed extensions selectively).

Role

Lead or co-lead with follow-on capacity.

Sectors

B2B SaaS, devtools, applied AI, fintech infra, vertical software, marketplaces.

  • Geography: EU/US first; APAC selectively.
  • Traction: from pre-revenue with design partners to early commercial traction.
  • Model: recurring or high-frequency transactional.
  • Moat: workflow lock-in, data/AI leverage, distribution edge, or regulatory wedge.

Signals We Look For

Clear founder–market fit, early proof of value, and a path to durable advantage.

Founder–Market Fit

Relentless, product-oriented founders with unique insight or unfair access.

01.
Details
  • Evidence of speed: shipping cadence, roadmap velocity.
  • Learning loop: tight build–measure–learn cycles.
  • Magnetism: ability to attract talent/design partners.

Engaged Users

Retention over vanity. We value depth of usage and strong feedback signal.

02.
Details
  • Cohorts: logo retention & expansion trends.
  • Payback: < 12 months preferred for B2B SaaS.
  • Pipeline: signed LOIs, pilots, or repeat usage.

Unit Economics

Early signs of efficiency: CLV/CAC logic, gross margin >70% for SaaS.

03.
Details
  • Pricing story tied to value created.
  • Scalable distribution: product-led, partner, or outbound fit.
  • Path to 60%+ contribution margins at scale.

Moat & Edge

Data/AI compounding, workflow lock-in, network effects, or regulated wedge.

04.
Details
  • Hard-to-replicate datasets or integrations.
  • Switching costs & workflows embedded in ops.
  • Compliance/infra advantages others avoid.

03.

Our Process & Timeline

Fast, founder-friendly, and decisive. First response within two business days.

Step 1 — Intro & Deck

Send your deck + metrics link. If there’s a fit, we schedule a 20–30 min intro.

Step 2 — Light DD

Product demo, cohorts, pipeline, pricing, and hiring plan.

Step 3 — Work Session

We jam on roadmap, GTM, and edges we can unlock together.

Step 4 — Term Sheet

Simple, standard terms. We aim to be first-call and long-term.

Step 5 — Close & Build

Hands-on post-investment: hiring, pricing, partnerships, and intros.

Speed & expectations

  • First response within two business days.
  • Light diligence: product demo, cohorts, pipeline, pricing
  • Fast, standard terms when there’s a clear fit

Light data room

  • Deck + product demo (loom ok)
  • MRR & cohorts (screenshot or read-only link)
  • Top 10 customers & pipeline
  • Org plan & key hires next 6–12 months

04.

FAQs & Exclusions

Clear expectations help everyone move faster.

Do you lead?

Yes, we often lead or co-lead and help assemble the round. We also do selective follow-ons.

Board seats?

Lightweight governance. Board seat only when helpful to the founder and the company.

Geography?

Primarily EU/US focus. We consider APAC if there’s a strong edge or presence.

Exclusions

We do not invest in: gambling, weapons, adult content, predatory lending, or projects misaligned with our ethics.

  • Beyond capital: hiring support, pricing & packaging, enterprise sales playbooks, partner/intros, fundraising help.
