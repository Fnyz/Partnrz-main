01

Is there a fit?

Fast, founder-friendly, and decisive. If there's a fit, you'll hear from us quickly.

Stage

Pre-seed to Series A (seed extensions selectively).

Role

Lead or co-lead with follow-on capacity.

Focus

B2B SaaS, devtools, applied AI, fintech infra, vertical software, marketplaces.

Geography

EU/US first; APAC selectively.

Quick response

We aim for a first response within two business days. Share enough context so we can move fast.

01.

What to include

Short and sharp is perfect. Links are fine.

  • Concise company overview (problem → solution → why now → differentiation).
  • Short Loom product demo (or public demo URL).
  • Website + docs or GitHub (if relevant).
  • A few key metrics (users, revenue or pilots, growth) + round details & target close timing.
  • Optional: light data room (MRR/cohorts screenshot, top customers/pipeline, org plan & near-term hires).

03.

Our process & timeline

Short, structured, and collaborative.

Step 1 — Intro & deck

Submit your deck + links. If there's a fit, we schedule a 20–30 min intro.

Step 2 — Light DD

Product demo, cohorts, pipeline, pricing, and hiring plan.

Step 3 — Work session

We jam on roadmap, GTM, and edges we can unlock together.

Step 4 — Term sheet

Simple, standard terms. We aim to be first-call and long-term.

Step 5 — Close & build

Hands-on post-investment: hiring, pricing, partnerships, and intros.

Speed & expectations

  • First response within two business days.
  • Light diligence: product demo, cohorts, pipeline, pricing.
  • Fast, standard terms when there's a clear fit.

Questions?

Prefer email? Reach us at hello@partnrz.com.

04.

FAQs & exclusions

Clear expectations help everyone move faster.

Do you lead?

Yes, we often lead or co-lead and help assemble the round. We also do selective follow-ons.

Board seats?

Lightweight governance. Board seat only when helpful to the founder and the company.

Geography?

Primarily EU/US focus. We consider APAC if there's a strong edge or presence.

Exclusions

We do not invest in: gambling, weapons, adult content, predatory lending, or projects misaligned with our ethics.

  • Beyond capital: hiring support, pricing & packaging, enterprise sales playbooks, partner/intros, fundraising help.
