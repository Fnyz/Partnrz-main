Privacy
Policy

How Partnrz BV collects, uses, and protects personal data. Built for EU/GDPR compliance.

01

Overview & Controller

We explain what personal data we process, why, and how you can exercise your rights.

  • Controller: Partnrz BV (“Partnrz”, “we”, “us”), The Netherlands.
  • Contact: privacy@partnrz.com
  • Scope: this website and our Pitch process.
  • Last updated: September 5, 2025

We may update this policy periodically by posting a new version on this page.

01.

Data we collect

We collect only what we need to evaluate pitches, run this site, and communicate with you.

  • Pitch data: name, email, company, website, deck/demo links, metrics, round details, message.
  • Technical data: IP address, user agent, timestamps, basic logs for security/rate-limiting.
  • Cookies: essential cookies for site functionality; optional analytics cookies only with your consent (see our Cookie Policy).
  • Communications: messages and correspondence you send us.
02.

Use & legal bases

We process personal data for specific purposes under EU/UK GDPR legal bases.

  • Evaluate pitches & communicate — legitimate interests; contract (pre-contractual steps).
  • Security & abuse prevention — legitimate interests; legal obligation where applicable.
  • Operate & improve the site — legitimate interests.
  • Analytics/measurement — consent (only if you opt in to analytics cookies).
  • Legal/compliance — legal obligation; legitimate interests.
03.

Retention & sharing

We keep data only as long as needed and share it responsibly.

  • Retention: pitch submissions are retained for evaluation and internal records; logs are kept for security and troubleshooting; we periodically review and delete data that is no longer needed.
  • Recipients: service providers under contract (e.g., hosting, security, analytics if consented). We do not sell personal data.
  • International transfers: if data is processed outside the EEA/UK, we use appropriate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses) or rely on adequacy decisions when available.
04.

Your rights

You can exercise data protection rights subject to legal limits.

    Access & portability — request a copy of your data.
  • Rectification & erasure — correct or delete inaccurate/outdated data.
  • Restriction & objection — restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Consent withdrawal — withdraw analytics cookie consent at any time.
  • Complaint: you may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in NL: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Security, children & changes

  • Security: we use technical and organizational measures to protect data, but no method is 100% secure.
  • Children: this site is not directed to children; do not submit children’s data.
  • Changes: if we materially change this policy, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you.
  • Contact: privacy@partnrz.com
